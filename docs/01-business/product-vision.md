# ZenAI Prep — Product Vision

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Product:** ZenAI Prep
**Domain:** zenaiassist.com
**Initial Exam:** CELPIP General
**Business Model:** SaaS

## 1. Vision

Build a trusted, AI-powered exam preparation platform
that helps students practice effectively, understand
their weaknesses, and improve their performance.

ZenAI Prep will initially focus on CELPIP General
and later expand to IELTS, PTE, GRE, GMAT, SAT,
and professional certification exams.

## 2. Mission

Make personalized, high-quality exam preparation
accessible, affordable, and measurable.

## 3. Target Customers

- Immigration applicants preparing for language exams
- International students
- Working professionals pursuing certifications
- Repeat test takers seeking targeted improvement
- Tutors and educational institutions (future B2B)

## 4. Core Product Capabilities

- Realistic timed practice tests
- AI-powered writing and speaking feedback
- Reading and listening assessments
- Personalized learning recommendations
- Progress tracking and analytics
- Full mock examinations
- Subscription-based access

## 5. Competitive Differentiation

ZenAI Prep will focus on actionable feedback rather
than simply generating questions or scores.

Key differentiators:

1. Evidence-based AI evaluation
2. Personalized improvement recommendations
3. Consistent practice and progress tracking
4. Transparent, unofficial score estimates
5. Support for multiple exam categories over time

## 6. Business Objectives

### Short Term
- Deliver a working CELPIP MVP
- Validate demand with early users
- Establish scoring quality benchmarks
- Launch a limited commercial beta

### Medium Term
- Expand CELPIP practice content
- Introduce subscription plans
- Add IELTS and PTE
- Improve AI evaluation accuracy

### Long Term
- Become a global multi-exam preparation platform
- Introduce institutional and tutor subscriptions
- Support academic and professional certifications
- Develop proprietary learning analytics

## 7. Product Principles

- Student outcomes first
- Privacy and security by design
- Transparent AI limitations
- High-quality original or licensed content
- Cost-efficient and scalable engineering
- Accessibility and inclusive design

## 8. Success Metrics

- Student activation and retention
- Practice completion rates
- Writing and speaking feedback quality
- Measurable improvement across attempts
- Free-to-paid conversion
- Customer satisfaction
- Sustainable unit economics

## 9. Initial Technical Strategy

- Next.js frontend
- FastAPI backend
- PostgreSQL database
- Managed authentication
- Asynchronous AI evaluation in later sprints
- Vercel for frontend deployment

## 10. Branding

Customer-facing product: ZenAI Prep
Primary domain: zenaiassist.com

The ExamPilot AI name remains an internal
working reference until branding is finalized.

## 11. Disclaimer

ZenAI Prep is an independent exam preparation
platform. It is not affiliated with or endorsed
by official examination organizations.

AI-generated score estimates are unofficial.
